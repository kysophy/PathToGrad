# Stakeholders

This document identifies the stakeholders of the PathToGrad course planning system and describes each stakeholder's role and interest in the system.

| No. | Stakeholder | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Software Development Team | Works on the software and, in the current version, is also responsible for generating the data. |
| 2 | Registrar's Office and Database | In future development, the Registrar's Office will hold the academic records and define graduation rules. |
| 3 | Academic Advisors | Can see the current class list, review student plans and records, and identify possible conflict risks. This access allows them to guide students more precisely. |
| 4 | Department / Faculty Chairs | In future development, they will dictate the curriculum, prerequisites, and course availability, and decide on course changes. |
| 5 | Students | Main users of the software. They may plan anywhere from a single semester (checking prerequisites, availability, and schedules) to their full graduation path (tracking progress, GPA, and academic risks). |
| 6 | Parents | Have no direct access to the software, but are guardians of students with a direct financial investment (tuition) in the results the system produces. |
